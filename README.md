# FarmersDelightBedrock

FarmersDelightBedrock is a Minecraft mod that adds a variety of Category 1 and Category 2 plants to the game. Category 1 plants grow similarly to wheat, are edible, and restore between 1 and 5 hunger points, providing 1 to 6 saturation points. They are generated within garden structures and can also appear in a "wild" state on grass blocks. Category 2 plants are trees that grow like standard trees, with each leaf block having a 100% chance to drop 1–3 pieces of fruit and an 8% chance to drop a sapling. The mod includes plants like corn, tomato, hot red pepper, green bell pepper, greens, olive, banana, apple, peach, orange, lemon, ironwood, and plum.

## Install

1. Download the mod from the GitHub repository.
2. Place the downloaded `.jar` file into the `mods` folder of your Minecraft instance.
3. Launch Minecraft with the Fabric loader installed.

## Use

1. Find and plant the seeds for Category 1 plants in your world.
2. Harvest the mature plants to obtain the fruits.
3. Consume the fruits to restore hunger and saturation points.
4. Find and plant the saplings for Category 2 plants in your world.
5. Harvest the fruits from the leaves of the mature trees.
6. Consume the fruits to restore hunger and saturation points.
